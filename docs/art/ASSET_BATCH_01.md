Part of the [Style Bible](STYLE_BIBLE.md). Style suffix version: **v1**. Hemp guards version: **v1**.

**17 images.** Ordered by what the World 1 vertical slice needs first: rooms for the first five floors, the hemp crop and its floor, workers who can move, managers, a chest opening, cards, then the first hemp product floor and the menu icons. If you only have time for ten, do prompts 1 to 10.

Everything here avoids final names. Where a name or list could still change, the prompt's **Name flag** says so.

## How to do it on your phone

1. The work is split into **6 rounds**. Each round is **one new ChatGPT chat**.
2. At the start of a round, **attach that round's reference images** from your own packs (two images, or three in round 3, listed below) and paste the round's first prompt in the same message.
3. Then paste the round's other prompts **one at a time, in the same chat**. You do not need to attach the images again.
4. Each prompt is one grey box. Copy the whole box, change nothing.
5. Save each result as it comes: **PNG, original size**. No screenshots, no JPG, no cropping or resizing.
6. Upload the results to the project. You do not need to rename them. Upload in number order, or tell me which number each one is.
7. If a result looks wrong, use the matching sentence from "If a result is off" near the bottom. If it is still off after two tries, upload it anyway and tell me. A lot can be fixed in clean-up.
