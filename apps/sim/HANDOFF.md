## Assumptions (also in the report)

- New-player model: 0.3 purchases a second and 30 percent of taps landed for 2 minutes, then 0.35 and 35 percent to minute 10, 0.4 and 35 percent to the end of the first hour, then 2 a second and every tap.
- Abilities only while their world is on screen; leaving a world ends Combo lock and Liftoff. Tea Party adds 120 s of steady income to the bubbles; Wake-Up Call makes the next missed sunrise pay as caught. Auto-Gardener is one switch; Head Start money on first entry; purchases do not interrupt tapping; Autopilot taps are not counted taps. Garden Fair every weekend.
